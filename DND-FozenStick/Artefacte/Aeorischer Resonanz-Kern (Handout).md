### Aeorischer Resonanz-Kern – Dormant State (Common)
_Wondrous Item (Prosthetic Core), Common (requires attunement by Gimbal)_

A fist-sized sphere of dull, grey-violet crystal, cold to the touch and utterly weightless. Deep inside, something beats slowly, in perfect rhythm with your own heart. You recovered it from the shattered domes of an Aeorian laboratory, where the scientists had locked these cores away in lead and warned against ever touching them with bare skin.

You gave it what it craved: a mechanical circuit. Built into the housing of your prosthetic arm, the core no longer gnaws at flesh. It hums. It pulls. And for the first time, the arm moves as if it were truly yours.

As long as you are attuned to this item and it remains seated in your prosthetic, the core is stable. Removed from its housing, it begins to hunger again.

**Mass Calibration** At the end of a long rest, you can recalibrate the core into one of two modes. The chosen mode lasts until you change it again.

- **Burden Mode:** The core drives mass _into_ the arm. It strikes like an anvil. Your **Strength score increases by 2** (to a maximum of 20).
    
- **Drift Mode:** The core strips the arm of its mass. It moves faster than thought. Your **Dexterity score increases by 2** (to a maximum of 20).
    

In Burden Mode, the core glows a deep, heavy indigo and the arm's joints creak under invisible weight. In Drift Mode, the light turns a pale violet, and small particles of dust and snow drift lazily upward around your hand.

**Core Slot** The core contains a small distillation chamber that works in rhythm with its pulse. When you finish a long rest, you can use your Alchemist's Supplies to create **one additional elixir** in this chamber. You choose one of the following:

- One **Core Elixir** whose recipe you know.
    
- One **Experimental Elixir**, rolled on your Experimental Elixir table as normal.
    

An elixir from the Core Slot lasts until it is drunk, thrown, or until the end of your next long rest. Any creature can use it. Where a Core Elixir calls for a saving throw, it uses **your spell save DC**.

**Known Core Recipes**

- **Gravity Vial** _(thrown)_: As an action, a creature can hurl this vial at a point it can see within **30 feet**. It shatters into a howling violet vortex. Each creature within a **5-foot radius** of that point must make a **Strength saving throw**. On a failed save, a creature takes **2d6 force damage** and is **pulled up to 10 feet** toward the center. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't pulled.
    
- **Orbit Elixir** _(drunk)_: For **1 minute** after drinking this elixir, small objects begin to circle the drinker in a slow, glittering orbit: coins, splinters, ice crystals. When the drinker is hit by a **ranged weapon attack**, they can use their **reaction** to pull the projectile into the orbit, reducing the damage by **1d10 + your Intelligence modifier**. If the damage is reduced to 0, the projectile joins the orbit until the effect ends.
    

_Sometimes, late at night, the pulse in the core skips a beat, as if it were listening for something very far away._
