### Aeorischer Resonanz-Kern
_Wondrous Item (Prosthetic Core), Artifact (requires attunement by Gimbal)_

A fist-sized sphere of dull, grey-violet crystal, cold to the touch and utterly weightless. Deep inside, something beats slowly, in perfect rhythm with your own heart. You recovered it from the shattered domes of an Aeorian laboratory, where the scientists had locked these cores away in lead and warned against ever touching them with bare skin.

You did not listen. Instead, you gave it what it craved: a mechanical circuit. Built into the housing of your prosthetic arm, the core no longer gnaws at flesh. It hums. It pulls. And for the first time, the arm moves as if it were truly yours.

### **Current State: Common**

_The core is stable, but barely awake. It answers your engineering, not yet your will._

- **Mass Calibration:** At the end of a long rest, you can recalibrate the core into one of two modes. The chosen mode lasts until you change it again.
    - **Burden Mode:** The core drives mass _into_ the arm. It strikes like an anvil. Your **Strength score increases by 2** (to a maximum of 20).
    - **Drift Mode:** The core strips the arm of its mass. It moves faster than thought. Your **Dexterity score increases by 2** (to a maximum of 20).
    
    - **Visual Effect:** In Burden Mode, the core glows a deep, heavy indigo and the arm's joints creak under invisible weight. In Drift Mode, the light turns a pale violet, and small particles of dust and snow drift lazily upward around your hand.

- **Core Slot:** The core contains a small distillation chamber that works in rhythm with its pulse. When you finish a long rest, you can use your Alchemist's Supplies to create **one additional elixir** in this chamber. You choose one of the following:
    - One **Core Elixir** whose recipe you know.
    - One **Experimental Elixir**, rolled on your Experimental Elixir table as normal.
    
    An elixir from the Core Slot lasts until it is drunk, thrown, or until the end of your next long rest. Any creature can use it. Where a Core Elixir calls for a saving throw, it uses **your spell save DC**.

#### **Known Core Recipes**

**Gravity Vial** _(thrown)_
As an action, a creature can hurl this vial at a point it can see within **30 feet**. It shatters into a howling violet vortex. Each creature within a **5-foot radius** of that point must make a **Strength saving throw**. On a failed save, a creature takes **2d6 force damage** and is **pulled up to 10 feet** toward the center. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn't pulled.

- **Visual Effect:** For a heartbeat, the world bends inward. Snow, blood and loose stones are sucked toward a single point, then drop to the ground as the vortex collapses with a dull, heavy thud.

**Orbit Elixir** _(drunk)_
For **1 minute** after drinking this elixir, small objects begin to circle the drinker in a slow, glittering orbit: coins, splinters, ice crystals. When the drinker is hit by a **ranged weapon attack**, they can use their **reaction** to pull the projectile into the orbit, reducing the damage by **1d10 + your Intelligence modifier**. If the damage is reduced to 0, the projectile joins the orbit until the effect ends.

- **Visual Effect:** A miniature solar system of debris spins around the drinker. Captured arrows and bolts circle with it, slowly tumbling, before falling to the ground when the minute ends.

### **The Path of Evolution**

_As the core awakens, it remembers what it was part of._

#### **Uncommon: The Waking Pulse**
- **Stronger Core Elixirs:** The Gravity Vial deals **3d6** force damage. The Orbit Elixir reduces damage by **1d12 + your Intelligence modifier**.
- **Massless Frame:** Any weapon you wield with the prosthetic gains the **Finesse** property. Armor you wear no longer imposes **disadvantage on Stealth** checks.

#### **Rare: The Second Chamber**
- **Second Core Slot:** You can create **two** additional elixirs with the core after a long rest.
- **Gravity Discharge:** Once per long rest, when you hit a creature with a melee attack using the prosthetic, you can release the stored mass: the target takes an extra **2d8 force damage** and must succeed on a Strength saving throw or be pushed **15 feet** and knocked **prone**.

#### **Very Rare: The Heavy Heart**
- **Mass Calibration:** The bonus of your chosen mode increases to a maximum of **22**.
- **Stronger Core Elixirs:** The Gravity Vial deals **4d6** force damage and pulls up to **15 feet**. The Orbit Elixir can also deflect **ranged spell attacks**.
