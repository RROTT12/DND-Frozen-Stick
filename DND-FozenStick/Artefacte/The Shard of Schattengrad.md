
_Wondrous Item (Amulet/Arcane Focus), Artifact (requires attunement by Patates)_

Found in your cradle amidst the chaos of Nebelfurt and your parents' final desperate act, this jagged, obsidian-like splinter pulses in perfect synchronization with your heartbeat. It is an anchor to the wild, entropic forces that destroyed your home. Under the guidance of your mentor, **Sisman Kurtarici**, you have learned to treat this relic not as a source of power, but as a regulator—a valve to control the "leak in reality" that resides within you.

### **Current State: Common**

_The shard acts as a bridge between your Cobalt Soul training and your chaotic bloodline. It is a reactive conduit, visually manifesting the energies you manipulate._

- **Arcane Resonance:** When you cast a spell through the shard normally, the matte black surface illuminates from within. The jagged edges glow with a steady light corresponding to the school of magic you are wielding—a deep crimson for Evocation, a shimmering silver for Divination, or a sickly green for Necromancy. It is a visual indicator of the raw arcane data flowing through your conduit.
    
- **The Chaos Valve:** The shard allows you to tap into your inner chaos in a controlled manner. It has **3 charges**. Whenever you cast a sorcerer spell of 1st level or higher, you can expend **1 charge** to roll on the **Wild Magic Surge table**. This replaces the standard d20 roll for Wild Magic.
    
    - **Visual Effect:** When you deliberately expend a charge to open the Valve, the reaction is violent. The shard doesn't just glow; it fractures internally. Blinding white fissures spiderweb across the obsidian surface, drowning out the school colors with raw, undiluted arcane potential. The air around you hums and warps, and for a brief second, the shard feels searing hot against your chest as reality bends.
        
- **Dawn’s Restoration:** The shard regains **all expended charges** daily at dawn.

### **The Path of Evolution**

As your understanding of the shard’s geometry and your own discipline grows, it will unlock the following capabilities:

#### **Uncommon: The Scholar's Focus**

_The shard resonates with your analytical training at the Central Archive._

- **Arcane Precision:** You gain a **+1 bonus** to spell attack rolls and your Spell Save DC.
    
- **Analytical Mind:** You have **advantage** on all **Intelligence** ability checks (Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion).
    

#### **Rare: The Echo of Lost Secrets**

_The splinter taps into the collective memory of the Age of Arcanum._

- **Arcane Precision:** The bonus to spell attacks and DC increases to **+2**.
    
- **Arcane Tap:** Once per long rest, you can use an action to cast **any spell from the Wizard spell list** without expending a spell slot. The spell must be of **3rd level or lower**. You do not need to know or prepare it.
    

#### **Very Rare: The Reinforced Conduit**

_Your mastery over the "leak" allows for deeper excavations of magic._

- **Arcane Precision:** The bonus to spell attacks and DC increases to **+3**.
    
- **Expanded Tap:** Your **Arcane Tap** feature can now cast Wizard spells of **5th level or lower**.
    

#### **Legendary: Sovereign of the Breach**

_You have mastered the balance between logic and entropy._

- **Arcane Precision:** The bonus remains **+3**.
    
- **Incorruptible Mind:** You gain **advantage on Intelligence saving throws** in addition to your Intelligence ability checks.
    
- **Grand Tapestry:** Your **Arcane Tap** feature can now cast Wizard spells of **7th level or lower**.
