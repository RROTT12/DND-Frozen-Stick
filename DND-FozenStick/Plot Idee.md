## Opt 1

### Phase 1: The Blood in the Snow (The Approach)

Don't let them reach the camp first—let them find Mila on their way back, just a mile or two outside the outpost.

- **The Scene:** As the party trudges through the snow, eager for a warm bed, the high-perception characters notice something wrong. A dark, jagged smear of red across the pristine white snow.
    
- **The Discovery:** They find Mila half-buried in a snowdrift. She is barely conscious, suffering from severe frostbite, and covered in horrific wounds that look like they were made by mutated claws.
    
- **The Item:** To make it hit hard, have Mila clutching something that belongs to Orvo—maybe his signature goggles, a broken compass, or his lucky flask—covered in blood.
    

### Phase 2: A Ghost Town (The Arrival)

When the party carries Mila into the camp, the usual bustling atmosphere is dead.

- **The Atmosphere:** Tents are empty, fire pits are cold. The silence is deafening.
    
- **The Reception:** Only **Morgo** and **The Buyer** come running out. The Buyer is thrilled to see the Salsvault vials (mission accomplished!), but the celebration is instantly cut short when they see Mila's state.
    
- **The Panic:** Morgo takes charge of treating Mila, looking grim. The Buyer explains the situation: _“A massive distress flare went up from a newly uncovered ruin a few days ago. Everyone went to claim the glory. When no one came back, Orvo, the dwarf, and Mila went to rescue them. You are the only ones left.”_
    

### Phase 3: Mila’s Warning (The Lore Drop)

Once Mila is stabilized (perhaps the party uses magic to heal her), she wakes up in a panic. She grabs a party member's arm and delivers the chilling truth:

- **The Ambush:** The first wave of adventurers wasn't killed by the cold or traps; they were slaughtered by monstrous beasts.
    
- **The Villain Introduction:** _“It wasn't wild... they were organized. Commanded.”_ Mila explains that when they arrived, a figure stepped out of the shadows. **The Tamer**.
    
- **The Sacrifice:** She tearfully explains how easily The Tamer's beasts ripped Orvo apart. The dwarf, already badly wounded, used his last bit of strength to cause a cave-in or block a doorway, screaming at Mila to run and warn the camp.
    

### Phase 4: The New Objective

The party now has a highly personal reason to go to this new lab.

1. **Avenge Orvo:** He was likely a friendly guide to them.
    
2. **Rescue the Dwarf:** There is a slim chance the dwarf is still alive, taken captive by The Tamer.
    
3. **Stop the Threat:** The Tamer now has an army of beasts and a whole camp's worth of dead adventurers' loot.


## opt 2 (Favorite)

### Phase 1: Der geknackte Manastein-Ring

Wenn die Gruppe das Camp betritt, blicken Morgo und The Buyer mit fahlen Gesichtern auf den Tisch im Kommandozelt.

- **Das Notsignal:** Auf dem Holztisch liegt ein Ring mit einem normalerweise klaren Manastein – das Gegenstück zu dem Ring, den der Zwerg bei sich trägt. Der Stein ist tief gerissen. Aus dem Riss sickert ein pulsierendes, ungesundes rotes Licht, und der Stein gibt ein leises, statisches Knistern von sich, das fast wie ein schwaches Stöhnen klingt.
    
- **Die Verzweiflung:** _"Das geht jetzt schon seit 18 Stunden so,"_ sagt Morgo leise. _"Etwas hat die Expedition in Stücke gerissen. Wir wussten, wir können niemanden mehr schicken, bevor ihr nicht da seid."_
    

### Phase 2: Das flackernde Licht im Schnee

Die Gruppe macht sich sofort auf den Weg. Nach einem harten Marsch durch den Schnee bemerkt jemand mit hoher passiver Wahrnehmung ein schwaches, unregelmäßiges Flackern in einer Schneewehe abseits des Weges.

- **Der Überlebenskampf:** Sie finden Mila. Sie ist halb erfroren und hat entsetzliche Kratz- und Bisswunden, die von etwas Riesigem stammen müssen.
    
- **Das Artefakt am Limit:** An ihrem Hals hängt der Anhänger. Er erschafft keine bequeme "Blase", sondern kämpft verzweifelt gegen den Tod an. Der Stein im Anhänger flackert wild und gibt ein angestrengtes, hohes Summen von sich. Seine Magie hat sich in ihre Wunden gefressen und das Blut zu einer Art magischem Eis oder Bernstein kristallisiert, um sie am Verbluten zu hindern. Mila ist kaum noch bei Bewusstsein, ihr Atem geht nur noch extrem flach. Der Anhänger ist kurz davor, auszubrennen.
    
- **Rettung:** Erst wenn die Spieler eigene Heilmagie oder Tränke anwenden, dimmt das Licht des Anhängers langsam herunter – nicht weil es abgeschaltet wird, sondern weil die Magie merkt, dass der akute Sterbeprozess gestoppt ist (oder er zerspringt leise, weil er völlig überlastet war).
    

### Phase 3: Die namenlose Bedrohung

Mila schlägt die Augen auf. Sie ist traumatisiert, friert erbärmlich und krallt sich an die Spieler. Sie kennt keinen "Tamer", sie weiß nur, dass das, was dort draußen ist, kein normaler Monsterangriff war.

- **Die grausige Wahrheit:** _"Da waren... Bestien. Aber sie waren nicht wild. Sie haben uns nicht einfach angefallen."_ Sie hustet schwer. _"Sie haben uns in eine Falle getrieben. Flankiert."_
    
- **Die Gestalt:** _"Jemand hat sie befehligt. Eine Gestalt... ich konnte das Gesicht im Schneesturm nicht sehen. Aber als sie die Hand hob, haben die Kreaturen gestoppt. Als sie auf Orvo zeigte... haben sie ihn einfach zerrissen. Wer auch immer das war... diese Person kontrolliert die Monster."_
    
- **Das Opfer des Zwergs:** Sie weint bitterlich, als sie erzählt, wie der Zwerg sich schwer blutend in einen felsigen Engpass stellte und mit seiner Waffe den Eisüberhang zum Einsturz brachte, um die Bestien von ihr abzuschirmen, damit sie Hilfe holen konnte.


### Beat 1: The Chilling Silence (The Arrival)

Instead of walking straight to the tent, let them experience the dread as they walk through the camp.

- **The Atmosphere:** As the party approaches the outpost, the usual smell of roasting meat and loud boasting of mercenaries is absent. Only a few guards are visible, huddled near small fires, looking nervously toward the horizon.
    
- **The Greeting:** No one cheers. A guard simply nods and points toward the main command tent: _"Morgo and the Buyer are waiting. It’s bad."_ This instantly sets off alarm bells for the players.
    

### Beat 2: Securing the Cure (The Buyer's Relief)

When they enter the tent, Morgo is pacing, and the Buyer looks exhausted.

- **The Hand-off:** When the players reveal the vials of the Frigid Woe cure, the Buyer actually slumps in his chair with relief. _"Thank the gods. You actually survived Salsvault."_
    
- **The Courier:** To reassure the players that their quest is complete, the Buyer doesn't just say he'll send it—he acts immediately. He calls in a heavily cloaked, elite Aarakocra courier (or uses a rare _Scroll of Teleportation_ he was hoarding). He packs the vials securely right in front of them. _"This leaves for Palebank Village right now. Your people will be cured. Your contract for Salsvault is fulfilled. But... we have a new nightmare."_
    

### Beat 3: The Broken Ring (The Investigation)

The Buyer steps aside, revealing the broken Manastone ring on the central map table. This is where you let the players interact and roll some dice.

- **The Visual:** The silver band is snapped. The blue crystal is fractured, bleeding a sickly, pulsing red light that crackles like static.
    
- **Arcana / Investigation Check (DC 13):** If a player examines it, they learn that the magic connecting it to its twin ring (worn by the Dwarf) is hanging by a literal thread. The red pulse is essentially a magical "flatline" warning. If they roll high (16+), they can actually feel a faint directional pull from the ring—it wants to reconnect with its twin.
    
- **The Briefing:** Morgo explains the timeline. _"A massive distress flare went up from a newly uncovered Aeorian dome 18 hours ago. Everyone went. Nobody came back. We sent Mila, Orvo, and your Dwarf to pull them out. Now... this ring is doing _this_. We are completely blind."_
    

### Beat 4: The Desperate Request & Outfitting

The Buyer looks at the party. _"You are the only veterans left. If anyone is still breathing out there, it's because that Dwarf is too stubborn to die. But you need to leave now."_

- **The Snow-Wargs:** He leads them outside to four heavily muscled, thick-coated Snow-Wargs. They are terrifying but tamed. **Animal Handling Check (DC 12):** Ask the players to roll to see how well they bond with their mount. A success means the Warg is fiercely loyal; a failure means the Warg occasionally nips at their boots or groans, adding some fun roleplay flavor. The Wargs will cut the travel time down to mere hours.
    
- **The Comms:** Morgo presses a _Sending Stone_ (or a flare gun) into the party leader's hand. _"The moment you know what happened, use this. And take the broken ring—let it guide you to the Dwarf."_ By breaking it down like this, your players get a satisfying conclusion to their Frigid Woe quest, a chance to use their skills to examine the clues, and a fun new toy (the Wargs) before they head back out into the cold.
