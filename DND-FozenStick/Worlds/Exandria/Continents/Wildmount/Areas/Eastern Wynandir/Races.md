### the Kryn Dynasty / Xhorhas (Eastern)

_A vast wasteland where "monstrous" races have built a sophisticated, spiritual civilization._

- **Dominant:** Drow (Dark Elves), Orcs, Goblins (and Hobgoblins/Bugbears).
    
- **Common:** Tieflings, Hollow Ones (undead-like humans), Gnolls.
    
- **Present:** Minotaurs, Centaurs, Kobolds.
    
- **Goliaths:** Nomadic tribes in the **Ashkeeper Peaks** (the mountain range separating East and West).
